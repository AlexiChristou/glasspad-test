Chords
Rhythm is how the chords are played in time: held (Sustain, or Swell to fade each one in), on the beat, 8ths, off-beat stabs, Pushes (the next chord comes in early), Charleston, Latin 3-3-2, Gallop, Trance gate (choppy 16ths) or Half-time. Strum rolls each chord's notes like a guitar. Pump makes the chords duck on every kick and swell back (and the bass a little), the breathing sound of synth music. Feel plays the chords more like a person: hits a touch early or late, strong and weak beats, the odd hit left out, chords moving to nearby shapes, harder hits brighter, and leaning on the kick and snare. Tight is subtle, Natural in between, Loose relaxed. In Pro, Feel details lets you switch each part and set the amount. In Pro, the Arpeggiator plays the chord one note at a time: "Every hit" follows the rhythm, or pick a speed.
The chords come from your song.

My sounds
Play with
Tap Record with mic and hum, sing or make a sound (up to 4 seconds), or add a sound file. Short sounds work best: a word, a clap, a hum. Up to 8, each trimmed to 2.5 seconds. Each sound can be for the Lead, the Bass or Both; Split and Cycle use just that part's sounds. Note length Even: low notes stop at the clip's own length. Stretched: every note keeps the full clip, with a grainy texture (tight is the sharper, original version). Natural: low notes play slower and longer, like tape. Repeat while held (or the lead's Hold) repeats on the beat: "Repeats: next sound" steps through your sounds each time, "same sound" keeps repeating the one you touched.

Start fresh stops everything, clears the loop and puts every setting back to the start. Your saved presets, saved loops and sounds are kept. Pad size sets how tall the pad stays while the controls are open (the controls scroll in the space above). Lift pad raises the pad off the bottom of the screen: some phones hold touches near the bottom edge for a moment, so if the outer bass keys respond late, lift it until they're instant. Smooth sound gives the sound engine a bigger buffer, which stops crackling on a busy phone but adds a little delay. Style sounds picks how the Quick start styles sound: Synth (electronic beats and synth chords) or Original (as they first were). Listening on: Phone speaker adds faint overtones above the bass so you can hear it on a phone's small speaker; Headphones plays the bass exactly as it is.
